A little about me

Technical thinking. Practical experience.

I'm studying BSc (Hons) Software Engineering at Manchester Metropolitan University, where I achieved a First Class result in my first year. I enjoy building things that are useful, straightforward and well put together.

Before university, I spent around a year on an IT support placement at Cromwell Tools, supporting a business with more than 1,000 staff. I handled user issues, hardware and software troubleshooting, device setup and some second-line tasks.

That experience taught me to listen carefully, explain technical issues clearly and keep people updated. I bring the same approach to website projects: understand the problem first, agree what needs doing and communicate throughout.
